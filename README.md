## STAR Reflection  

### Situation  
As part of my Work Readiness Training, I was required to participate in a mock interview that simulated a real job interview experience. The goal was to prepare us for future interviews and test our communication and presentation skills.  

### Task  
My task was to present myself as a professional ICT candidate, answer both behavioural and technical questions, and demonstrate my knowledge, confidence, and interpersonal skills throughout the session.  

### Action  
To prepare, I researched common ICT-related interview questions and practiced using the STAR method. During the interview, I discussed my main projects such as the Emergency24 mobile app and QueueEase system, explaining my role and the skills I developed. I focused on maintaining good posture, eye contact, and speaking clearly while expressing enthusiasm for the field.  

### Result  
The interviewer gave me positive feedback on my confidence and structure. I learned how to communicate my achievements more effectively and how to stay calm under pressure. This experience helped me identify small areas to improve, such as making my answers shorter and more focused. Overall, the mock interview gave me valuable insight into how to approach real interviews during my upcoming Work Integrated Learning (WIL) placement.
